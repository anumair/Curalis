What you get

Real-time availability

Slots update as they're booked. What you see is bookable.

Symptom intake

Your doctor reads your history before the consultation starts, not during it.

Plain-language summaries

Clinical notes rewritten so they make sense without a medical degree.

Medication reminders

Timed to your prescription, delivered to your inbox.

Calendar sync

Appointments land in your Google Calendar, and update themselves when plans change.

Email at every step

Confirmation, reminders, and immediate notice if anything changes.
